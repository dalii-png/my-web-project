## Reference Format

All verified references should follow a standard academic citation format, including:
- Author(s)
- Title
- Source (journal name, publisher, website)
- Year of publication
- Volume/issue (for journals)
- DOI or URL
- Access date

## Warning

Do NOT fabricate references. Do NOT include unverified references in the final report. Mark all references as "Verified" or "Pending Verification" with verification dates and notes.
